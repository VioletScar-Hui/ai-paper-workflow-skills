## Planned Skills

### paper-intake

Extract and normalize paper candidates from screenshots, copied lists, Markdown notes, CSV files, web pages, newsletters, DAIR Academy posts, or mixed Chinese-English paper lists.

Keywords: screenshot paper list, paper title extraction, messy paper list, Chinese paper titles, DAIR Academy, newsletter papers, 识别论文列表, 根据截图识别论文.

### paper-search

Resolve official paper titles, authors, dates, arXiv IDs, DOI links, GitHub repositories, project pages, model cards, and technical reports.

Keywords: arXiv lookup, DOI lookup, official paper title, research paper search, model card search, technical report lookup, 查论文, 找论文来源.

### paper-pdf-acquisition

Download, verify, rename, and organize research paper PDFs, including arXiv PDFs, GitHub technical reports, official model cards, and official pages saved as PDFs.

Keywords: PDF downloader, paper downloader, download AI papers, validate PDF, RAW folder, CSV manifest, 下载论文, 下载到RAW, 文件为PDF.

### paper-reading

Extract and explain paper sections, claims, methods, experiments, limitations, ablations, implementation details, and key contributions.

Keywords: paper reading, paper summary, paper analysis, explain this paper, 论文阅读, 论文总结, 论文拆解.

### paper-deep-dive

Produce structured single-paper deep dives with problem framing, method decomposition, novelty, evidence, limitations, and practical implications.

Keywords: paper deep dive, research analysis, implementation clues, paper critique, 论文深度分析, 精读论文.

### paper-comparison

Compare multiple papers by problem, method, benchmark, dataset, assumptions, limitations, novelty, and engineering usefulness.

Keywords: compare papers, paper table, research matrix, paper benchmark comparison, 对比论文, 横向比较论文.

### paper-synthesis

Synthesize many papers into literature reviews, taxonomies, research maps, timelines, trend reports, and open-problem lists.

Keywords: literature review, survey writing, research synthesis, paper taxonomy, research map, 文献综述, 研究脉络.

### paper-knowledge-base

Maintain Obsidian, Markdown, CSV, JSON, and manifest-based paper knowledge bases.

Keywords: Obsidian paper notes, Markdown research notes, paper database, paper index, knowledge base, 论文知识库, 论文索引.
